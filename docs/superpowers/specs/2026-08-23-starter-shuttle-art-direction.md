# Starter Shuttle — art direction and starter blueprint

**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Status:** Approved design, held for consumption at Tasks 13–15
**Consumed by:** Task 13 (ExteriorBuilder), Task 14 (InteriorBuilder), Task 15 (block resources + `_starter_grid()`)
**Supersedes:** Task 15 Step 1's "BoxMesh placeholders for every block", and Task 15 Step 5's 3×7 corvette

---

## 1. Why this document exists

The starting craft currently renders as `BoxMesh 8 × 4 × 12` with no material — literally an
untextured box. The fix is not to sculpt a hull. From Task 15 the exterior is generated by
`ExteriorBuilder` from the `ShipGrid`, and Task 15 Step 5 explicitly deletes any hand-built
`Hull` mesh.

**The whole design rests on one observation: a 1960s-television shuttlecraft is a chamfered box
with outboard engine pods.** That is exactly what a cube-and-wedge voxel builder produces
naturally. So the shuttle look decomposes cleanly into two things that are permanent:

1. **Block meshes** — `def.mesh` on six of the sixteen block resources.
2. **The starter blueprint** — the cell layout in `flight_test.gd::_starter_grid()`.

Neither is throwaway. Nothing in this document asks for a bespoke hull model.

### 1.1 What we take from the reference, and what we do not

We take the **silhouette language**: a flat-topped chamfered box, a raked nose carrying three
large windows, outboard engine pods, an off-white hull with a single red livery stripe, and a
stern hatch. That vocabulary is a genre convention and is what makes a box read as a small
crewed craft.

We do **not** reproduce a specific vessel: no registry numbers, no operator name lettering, no
faction insignia. Livery in Slice 1 is stripe colour only. Ship naming and markings come from
the game's own fiction later.

## 2. Envelope and proportions

Cell size is 2 m (`ShipGrid.CELL_SIZE`). Convention: **−Z is the bow**, +Y up, +X starboard —
matching `CanopyRemote` at `(0, 0.5, −5)` and `ChaseCamera` at `(0, 4, 18)` in the current scene.

| Axis | Cells | Metres |
|---|---|---|
| Beam (X) | 5 hull, plus one pod each side | 10 m hull, 14 m over pods |
| Height (Y) | 2 | 4 m |
| Length (Z) | 8 | 16 m |

Hull plan proportion is 10 : 16 ≈ 1 : 1.6, which matches the reference's plan view almost
exactly. Our craft is proportionally **flatter** than the reference (0.4 : 1 height-to-beam
versus roughly 0.7 : 1) because a 2 m cell forces a 2 m cabin and there is no half-height block.
That is accepted — flatter reads sleeker against starfield, which is what §11 of the slice spec
asks exteriors to do.

Two decks, and they do different jobs:

- **y = 0 — the cabin.** One open walkable volume, bow to stern. This is the whole interior.
- **y = +1 — the equipment deck.** Core, reactors, batteries, grav plating. Solid, not walkable.
  This is why the roof is flat, and it puts the ship's vital block where boarders in Slice 3 have
  to work for it.

## 3. The starter blueprint

Replaces `_starter_grid()` in Task 15 Step 5. Read the tables as plan views, bow at the top.

### 3.1 y = 0 — cabin

> **Superseded for the cabin interior** by docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-ship-interior-redesign-design.md §7.5 (2026-09-23): rows z = 0..2 are now
> a centreline corridor with five room blocks either side. The hull and every other cell are unchanged.

```
 z \ x     −2        −1         0          1         2
  −4       ·       canopy    canopy     canopy       ·
  −3     wedge      deck      deck       deck      wedge
  −2      hull      deck   pilot_seat    deck       hull
  −1      hull      deck      deck       deck       hull
   0      hull      deck      deck       deck       hull
  +1      hull      deck      deck       deck       hull
  +2      hull      deck      deck       deck       hull
  +3      hull    bulkhead   airlock   bulkhead     hull
```

The three `canopy` cells at z = −4 are the raked triple windshield, and they are the single
strongest identity cue in the whole design. The cabin is 3 cells wide — 6 m, exactly the width of
the hand-built room being replaced, so nothing about avatar movement needs re-tuning.

Walkable set: 18 `deck` + 1 `pilot_seat` + 1 `airlock` = 20 cells, fully connected bow to stern
along x ∈ {−1, 0, 1}. The pilot seat is `MOUNT`, so it does not break the aisle.

### 3.2 y = +1 — equipment deck and roof

```
 z \ x     −2        −1         0          1         2
  −4       ·         ·        wedge        ·         ·
  −3       ·       wedge      hull       wedge       ·
  −2     wedge      hull      hull       hull      wedge
  −1      hull      hull      core       hull       hull
   0      hull    reactor     hull     reactor      hull
  +1      hull  grav_plating  hull   grav_plating   hull
  +2      hull      hull      hull       hull       hull
  +3     wedge      hull      hull       hull      wedge
```

The tapering wedge cells at z = −4 and −3 form the brow over the windshield; the wedges at z = +3
close the stern. `core` sits amidships with hull on every face. Two grav plating units at
`grav_radius = 6.0` cover the 12 m cabin between them — deliberately legible, so a player who
later moves one can watch a section of deck go weightless.

### 3.3 y = 0, x = ±3 — engine pods

```
 (±3, 0, +1)  hull       pylon root
 (±3, 0, +2)  hull       pod body
 (±3, 0, +3)  thruster   engine bell
```

Flush outboard sponsons rather than gapped struts. A true gapped nacelle needs either an 18 m
beam or a sub-cell strut block; neither is worth it in Slice 1. Attachment to the main hull at
x = ±2 satisfies validation rule 2.

### 3.4 Acceptance criteria for the blueprint

Task 15 must verify these against real `ShipStats` output, not by eye:

- **All five validation rules pass, including rule 5.** Rough draw at these counts is about 8 MW
  of hotel load plus 4 × 3.0 MW of thrusters; two reactors at 12 MW each cover it with margin. If
  the real numbers land differently, change reactor count — a starter ship must not greet the
  player with a brownout warning.
- **`stats.induced_torque` under full forward burn is approximately zero.** Thrusters sit at
  y = 0 while the equipment deck puts mass at y = +1, so the centre of mass is above the thrust
  line and the ship will want to pitch under burn. Tune by moving equipment-deck mass or the pod
  row until it is balanced. The starter ship's first flight must be clean; deliberate imbalance is
  a lesson for ships the *player* builds.
- Block count lands near 80, well under the roughly 150 the spec budgets.

## 4. Block mesh specifications

Task 15 Step 1 currently says every block gets a `BoxMesh` placeholder. Six blocks carry the
shuttle read and need real meshes; the other ten stay boxes and change nothing.

| Block | Mesh |
|---|---|
| `hull` | 2 m cube, hard edges, slight bevel. Two surfaces: plate, and a recessed panel line. |
| `hull_wedge` | 2 m cube with one face chamfered 45°. All 24 `BlockOrientation` rotations reuse this one mesh — that is what produces every chamfer on the ship. |
| `canopy` | Wedge profile matching `hull_wedge` so the nose line is continuous, with the sloped face as dark glass. |
| `thruster` | The nacelle pod: a rounded 2 m tube, emissive bell on the aft face, small emissive dome forward. Two surfaces so the bell gets its own emissive material. |
| `airlock` | Hatch frame with a cyan emissive arch overhead — the aft doorway from the reference interior. Legible from across the cabin as "the way out", which pays off in Slice 3. |
| `pilot_seat` | Seat plus a raked forward console with emissive readout panels. |

**Constraint that shapes all of these:** `ExteriorBuilder` builds **one MultiMesh per block type**
with `mm.mesh = def.mesh`. There is no per-instance material. Any multi-material block must be an
`ArrayMesh` with one surface per material.

by colour and wear — cold or failing light, damage — not only by being darker.

The original text, for history: *Spec §11 says interiors are "dim and pooled". That applies to
derelict and enemy interiors in Slice 3, not to the player's own ship. The starter shuttle is
bright, warm and lived-in.*

## 6. The livery stripe

A red band running the hull's length is most of what separates a designed vessel from a pile of
cubes. It cannot be a per-block property: every `hull` cell shares one MultiMesh and one material.

**Solution: a `ShaderMaterial` on the hull and wedge meshes that paints the band from ship-local
position.** For a MultiMesh, `MODEL_MATRIX` already carries the per-instance transform, so
`(MODEL_MATRIX * vec4(VERTEX, 1.0)).y` in the vertex shader gives ship-local height. Paint
`livery_colour` where that falls inside a uniform band, hull colour elsewhere.

Three things fall out of this for free:

- Zero extra blocks, zero extra draw calls.
- The band follows whatever the player builds, on any hull, forever.
- `livery_colour` as a uniform means captured hulls in Slice 4 get instant faction identity from
  one colour value.

## 7. Amendments this design requires

Each of these is a change to an already-approved artefact. Listed so Task 15 does not have to
rediscover them.

1. **New sixteenth block: `canopy`** — Structure, `SOLID`, low mass, low hp (it is the weak point
   that makes Slice 2 shooting readable). Requires: slice spec §5 table 15 → 16 rows; Task 15
   Step 1 table gains a row; `test_all_fifteen_blocks_load` asserts 16 and is renamed.
2. **Task 15 Step 1** — replace "BoxMesh placeholders for every block" with §4 above for the six
   named blocks. The remaining ten stay boxes.
3. **Task 15 Step 5** — replace the 3 × 7 corvette `_starter_grid()` with §3 above.
4. **Task 14, InteriorBuilder** — a walkable cell whose face touches a `canopy` cell gets the
   canopy `SubViewport` material instead of a wall. This *generalises* the hand-built `Window`
   node that exists today rather than special-casing it, and is what makes the three bow windows
   visible from the pilot seat. *Since the interior redesign (2026-09-23), canopy faces keep only
   their colliders; the visible canopy is the generated rounded nose, whose windows project the
   canopy view by direction from the pilot's eye (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-ship-interior-redesign-design.md §6).*
5. **Task 14, InteriorBuilder** — `_build_floors()` and `_build_walls()` currently emit
   `CollisionShape3D` only. They must also emit meshes on **render layer 2**. This is BLOCKER-A
   from Task 6b (interior had colliders and no visuals) and it will recur verbatim unless Task 14
   is written to avoid it.
6. **Task 13, ExteriorBuilder** — `MultiMeshInstance3D.layers` must remain 1 so the canopy
   `SubViewport` (`cull_mask = 1`) renders the hull. It currently defaults correctly; do not
   change it.
7. **`flight_test.tscn`** — `ChaseCamera` at `(0, 4, 18)` frames a 12 m hull. The shuttle is 16 m
   long over a 14 m beam; move to roughly `(0, 5, 26)`.

## 8. Deliberate non-goals

- **Colliders stay full 2 m boxes**, including under wedge and canopy cells. The Task 13 parity
  test requires exactly one collider per occupied cell. A chamfered nose that collides as a box is
  correct for Slice 1 — do not "fix" it.
- **No gapped nacelle struts.** See §3.3.
- **No decals, registry text, or insignia.** See §1.1.
- **No bench seating block.** The reference interior's crew bench is attractive and is a natural
  seventeenth block with real Slice 3 use (crew and prisoner seating), but it is not needed to
  make the starter craft read as a shuttle. Deferred, not forgotten.
