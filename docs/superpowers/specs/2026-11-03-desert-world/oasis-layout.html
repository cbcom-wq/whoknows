The oasis, as I imagine it

Top-down sketch, roughly 150 m across. Everything here is my guess: mark what's wrong.

1 Landing pad. Packed stone, flat and clear: where you set the ship down.
2 The pool, ringed with palms and green. The one wet, living spot.
3–5 Homes: mud-brick with cloth shade sails. You can walk into two of them.
6, 7 Prefab modules the settlers flew in, patched with local brick. One is the workshop, one the comms hut with the last log.
8 A crushed hut. Roof caved in: the first sign something big came through.
9 The broken wall, knocked through from the desert side, with huge tracks heading off into the dunes.
10 A rock outcrop to the north: shade, and a lookout over the dunes.
11 Garden plots, watered by a pump at the pool, half dried out.
