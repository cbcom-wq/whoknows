Coming down to a desert world

Today first, then a rough guess at the descent in four stages. Painted sketches, not renders. React to the feel, not the pixels.

Today

standing on any world
  • Black sky and stars right down to the ground. A hard white sun.
  • Space dust hangs in the air over the sand.
  • Far rocks are as sharp as near ones. Nothing says "air".
  • Silent. It feels like a big asteroid.

1 · Approach

far out, falling in
  • The world has a soft warm glow round its edge: you can see it has air before you arrive.
  • Still space: stars, dust, silence.

2 · Into the air

high up, the sky starts
  • Black turns deep blue overhead, with a warm band along the curved horizon.
  • Stars fade out. The space dust thins and is gone.
  • The ground below is soft and hazy, not crisp.
  • A faint rush of wind starts up.

3 · Flying low

a kilometre or so up
  • A full sky: pale blue overhead, sandy haze at the horizon, a soft glow round the sun.
  • Far mesas fade into the haze, near ones are strong and warm: you can judge distance.
  • Wind noise rises with speed.

4 · On the ground

stepping out of the airlock
  • Big warm sky. The sun is soft and golden, not a white dot.
  • Sand blows in thin streaks; spiky dry grass, scattered rocks.
  • Mesas in the haze; the oasis a glint of water and palms far off.
  • You hear wind. It feels like standing outside, not on a rock in space.
